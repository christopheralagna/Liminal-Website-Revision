## 2. Mission, Philosophy & Guiding Principles

### The Definition of "Liminal"
> *"The word 'liminal' describes something that is difficult to define or categorize because it exists in-between things that are more familiar to us."*
> — **Source:** Squarespace Story page (`Screenshot 2026-11-07 at 4.05.05 PM.png`) & Original website (`about.html`)

### Founding Purpose & Critique of Commercial Spaces
> *"Liminal was conceived while confronting the harsh reality that our society is fundamentally driven by profit. Under the rhetorical facade of 'freedom' is the right of the owning-class to exploit working-class labor and to protect their private wealth... Trying to make sense of the way that mindsets are shaped by neoliberal capitalism, two Flagstaff locals discussed feeling surrounded by creative stagnation when it came to practical community-based social alternatives; There had to be a multitude of structural possibilities for innovative public spaces which prioritize people over profit."*
> — **Source:** Squarespace Story page (`Screenshot 2026-11-07 at 4.05.05 PM.png`)

### The Role of a Cafe in an Arts Center
From Christopher Alagna's essay *"Why should there be a cafe in a community arts center?"* (Jan 27, 2021, `references/original-website/liminal-website-master/blog.html`):
- Coffeeshops are familiar community anchors, but commercial cafes frequently exclude working-class and marginalized patrons through high prices and soft or hard policing.
- Liminal sought to adopt what fosters community connection while actively replacing exclusionary profit motives with genuine community solidarity and a radical open-door policy.

### Popular Education & De-Commodified Learning
From `references/original-website/liminal-website-master/classes+workshops.html`:
- Grounded in Brazilian educator Paulo Freire's **Popular Education** theory (*Pedagogy of the Oppressed*).
- Rejecting the belief that valuable learning only occurs within debt-inducing, hierarchical corporate universities.
- Centering peer-to-peer knowledge sharing where community members teach one another practical, political, and creative skills without requiring professional gatekeeping or certification.

## 3. Physical Facilities & Community Resources

Verified by Squarespace screenshots (`references/squarespace-photos/about/our-facilities/`):

1. **The Cafe Bar**:
   - Offered third-wave pour-overs, espresso drinks, house-blended teas, and cold pre-packaged foods from local partner *Evolve Eats*.
   - Free barista job skills training for volunteers.
   - Operating on a sliding-scale and pay-what-you-can donation basis.
2. **The Art Supply Room**:
   - Stocked with high-quality donated and purchased mediums: acrylic paints, spray paint, markers, colored pencils, drawing paper, and textiles.
   - Completely free for community members to use during open hours.
3. **The Radical Library & Zine Archive**:
   - Curated collection of books and grassroots zines focusing on anti-capitalism, feminist theory, anti-racism, mutual aid, indigenous sovereignty, and labor history.
   - Free reading on-site with plans for a community lending system.
4. **The Multimedia & Event Space**:
   - Multi-purpose room equipped with audiovisual equipment, digital projector, round-table seating, and flexible layout.
   - Daytime workspace and study room; evening event venue for documentary screenings, workshops, meetings, and live music.
5. **Harm Reduction Distribution Station**:
   - Provided free, low-barrier access to Narcan (Naloxone nasal spray), fentanyl testing strips, safer-use supplies, and hygiene kits.
   - Co-facilitated with regional harm reduction organizations like Sonoran Prevention Works (SPW).

## 6. Archival Tone & Editorial Guidelines

1. **Clear Historical Retrospective Stance**:
   - The public website must prominently communicate that Liminal is a former physical space that operated from 2021 to 2023.
   - Use warm, dignified past-tense verbs for past operations: *"Liminal operated...", "The cafe served...", "Volunteers organized..."*
   - Avoid misleading active phrasing: do not use *"Visit us today"*, *"Donate to keep our doors open"*, or active hours widgets that imply doors are unlocked tomorrow.
2. **Prominent Archive Header & Banner**:
   - A clear banner on every public page:
     > *"This website is an interactive digital archive commemorating Liminal Community Center & Cafe (Flagstaff, AZ, 2021–2023). It preserves the history, art, and mission of the physical space while demonstrating modern full-stack web engineering."*
3. **Respect for Historic Dignity**:
   - Preserve authentic community photos, flyers, and texts without sensationalizing or sanitizing the radical political and community mission.
   - Preserve quotes faithfully with attribution.
