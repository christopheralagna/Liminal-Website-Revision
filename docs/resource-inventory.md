# Liminal Resource Inventory & Assessment

This document provides a comprehensive inventory of all historical assets, website archives, operational documents, and media resources discovered in the project repository under `references/`.

---

## 1. Inventory Summary

The historical materials are organized into three primary reference collections:
1. **Squarespace Website Screenshots (`references/squarespace-photos/`)**: 35 PNG screenshots capturing the visual design, structure, layout, typography, colors, and content of the final production Squarespace website.
2. **Original Hand-Coded Website (`references/original-website/`)**: Complete source code of the initial static website built with HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, and Parcel bundler (`liminal-website-master/`), along with its compressed archive.
3. **Google Drive Archive (`references/google-drive-archive/`)**: Operational manuals, meeting notes, volunteer guides, event descriptions, schedules, legal/permit documentation, and high-resolution photo/video archives (both extracted and in `drive-download-20261107T232930Z-1-001.zip`).

### B. Original Hand-Coded Website (`references/original-website/liminal-website-master/`)
*Source code of the early static site authored by Christopher Alagna and Ryan McElligott.*

- **HTML Pages**:
  - `index.html`: Early home page structure, welcome text, mission introduction.
  - `about.html`: Detailed definitions of "The Name", "The Mission", "What We Offer", and theoretical grounding.
  - `cafe+menu.html`: Full historical drink and food menu with prices and the "pay high // pay low" community equity explanation.
  - `events.html`: Early event definitions, anti-capitalist popular education framework, opening night notice (March 27, 2021).
  - `classes+workshops.html`: Essay on alternative education, popular education (Paulo Freire), and de-commodifying learning.
  - `donate.html`: Early GoFundMe link, material donation categories (radical books, zines, art supplies, clothes).
  - `contact.html`: Contact form, location information, and volunteer inquiry notes.
  - `blog.html`: Two in-depth philosophical essays:
    1. *"Why should there be a cafe in a community arts center?"* by Christopher Alagna (Jan 27, 2021).
    2. *"Why am I Queer Before Anything Else? Identity Politics Eclipsing the Intended Value of Art"* by Ryan McElligott.
- **Styling & Assets**:
  - `css/main.css` & `css/about.css`: Original CSS rules, color palette (`#d4eac2`, `#575757`, `#1b1b1b`), typography (Montserrat, Georgia).
  - `css/images/`: 10 historical botanical and aesthetic image files (`aspens.jpg`, `books.jpg`, `coffeeshop.jpg`, `ferns-smaller.jpg`, `flowers.jpg`, etc.).
  - Favicon assets (`apple-touch-icon.png`, `favicon-32x32.png`, `site.webmanifest`).
- **Scripts & Config**:
  - `scripts/main.js`, `scripts/map.js`, `scripts/questions.js`: Interactive navigation, modal toggling, map embed script.
  - `package.json`: Parcel bundler configuration (`parcel *.html`).

### C. Google Drive Archive & Operational Documents

## 3. Duplication & Asset Overlaps

- **Photos**: Many images in `references/google-drive-archive/Liminal Media/General Photos/` are also contained within `drive-download-20261107T232930Z-1-001.zip`. The uncompressed folder contains identical hashes for key images (`SPW.JPG`, `bar1.jpg`, `liminalmainsign1.jpg`).
- **Event Flyers**: Several Canva flyers in `Liminal Event Photos/` exist in both PNG/JPG and landscape banner variants (e.g., `Renegade (Banner (Landscape)).png` vs `Renegade (Instagram Post).png`).
- **PDF vs DOCX**: Documents like `Event Descriptions` and `NEW Cafe Volunteer Schedule` exist in both Word (.docx) and exported PDF formats.

## 4. Privacy & Sensitive Information Flags

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Data Privacy Protocol**: The historical archives contain real-world personal, legal, and financial information. None of this data may be included in public website pages, checked into Git as seed data, or exposed in administrative demos.

The following specific files have been flagged for exclusion from public builds:
1. **Legal & Tax Records (Formerly in archive zip)**:
   - `Legal/fw9.pdf`, `Legal/w9.pdf`, `Legal/w9(1).pdf`: Contained private Taxpayer Identification Numbers (TIN), Social Security Numbers (SSN), legal entity details, and personal addresses.
2. **Volunteer & Community Intake Spreadsheets (Formerly in archive zip)**:
   - `Liminal Form Submissions/Volunteer Submissions/Volunteer Submissions.xlsx`
   - `Liminal Form Submissions/Workshop Submissions/Liminal Workshop Submission Responses.xlsx`
   - `Volunteer Info/Event Coordinator/Event Coordinator Signup.xlsx`
   - `Volunteer Info/Volunteer skills.xlsx`
   *These contained real volunteer contact information (personal phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses, and personal background statements).*

> [!NOTE]
> **Update**: `drive-download-20261107T232930Z-1-001.zip` was identified and subsequently purged from the repository. None of these sensitive files exist on disk in the workspace. All remaining files in `references/google-drive-archive/` are clean of personal tax forms or intake spreadsheets.
3. **Internal Governance & Credentials**:
   - Meeting notes containing personal discussions, private Slack invitation tokens, and banking/debit card holder references.

**Mitigation Plan**:
- All administrative demonstration databases will be seeded exclusively with **fictional, synthetic volunteer profiles, shifts, and financial records**.
- Public archive pages will reference only organizational public roles (e.g. Co-founders Christopher Alagna and Ryan McElligott, publicly listed performers/educators) and historical event dates already printed on publicly distributed flyers.
- Contact forms and volunteer applications on the modern website will submit to a secure sandboxed demonstration backend or mock handler, with explicit disclaimers that the organization is closed.
